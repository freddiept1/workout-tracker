# Workout Tracker

A simple workout log that runs in your web browser. Nothing to install.

## How to open it
Double-click `index.html`. It opens in your browser (Chrome or Edge work best). Bookmark that page so you can get back to it quickly.

The progress charts need an internet connection the first time the page loads. Everything else works offline.

## What it does
- **Log**: start from a template (Push / Pull / Legs) or a blank workout, then add sets with reps and weight. Tapping "+ Add set" copies the previous set.
- **History**: every workout you've saved, newest first. Tap one to see the details or delete it.
- **Progress**: pick an exercise to see a chart of your heaviest set each session.
- **Templates**: make your own routines. Put one exercise on each line.

## Your data
Your workouts are saved inside the browser on this computer. They don't go anywhere else. That means:
- They won't show up in a different browser or on your phone.
- Clearing your browser's history or site data can erase them.

**Back up regularly:** click **Export** (top right) to download a backup file. To restore it, click **Import** and pick that file. Importing replaces everything currently in the tracker.
